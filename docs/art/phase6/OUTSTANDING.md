# Phase 6 completion boundaries

## Delivered and usable for review/integration

- 27 individual RGBA runtime sprites: 14 newly generated static assets and 13 deterministic exports of existing art. Exact dimensions, frames, pivots, provenance and footprint reservations are in manifest.json.
- Eight new city starter modules across London, Lisbon and Mexico City; three new logistics objects; three new financial-difficulty props.
- Thirteen new vector UI icons, mappings for twelve economic event types and six shipment states; existing icons/badges reused where available.
- Nine executable business-state compositions, dynamically editable company name, six interactive reference routes, desktop/mobile captures, proposed typed view contracts and integration order.
- Native-scale city, logistics and business-state contact compositions; source originals and exact prompts.

## Engineering dependencies (outside this art branch)

International expansion quotes/eligibility, actual cross-city shipment lifecycle, freight/tariff ledger destinations, international recurring contracts, event-effect simulation, corporate groups/acquisitions/votes, geographic route data and news event feed are not established capabilities in the audited implementation. The UI fixture cannot enable them. Keep actions gated until Claude implements and tests authoritative contracts. Existing commodity trades are atomic; do not invent transit stock by drawing a truck.

This is a reviewable Phase 6 starter pack, not a declaration that every Phase 6 success criterion or the global economy is complete. Do not merge unreviewed art or treat illustrative fixture costs/effects as balancing requirements.
