# Phase 7 quality evidence

## Scope

Deterministic exports are inspected at native scale and 2× nearest neighbour, on dark and light grounds and beside the existing Bristol shop module. Initial review found farm-worker frame contamination where the scoop crossed equal-third source boundaries; the exporter now identifies three isolated alpha-column groups before framing. Contact sheets were rebuilt after that correction.

Structural checks pass 134 manifest records: source dimensions, frame bounds, RGBA transparency, nonempty frames, 24×40 world frames and [12,40] pivots. Work poses differ; rejected walk studies are not runtime assets. Exact individual files/checksums are listed in `files.csv`. These tests establish packing correctness, not strict native pixel-grid certification or artistic approval.

## Browser checks

`qa/browser-report.json` records the final results. Desktop 1440×1000 and mobile 390×844 capture all five routes, busy/quiet/closed/bankrupt/stale city states, dynamic branding, settled delivery replay, three owner reactions, empty filter, unavailable state-company report and article detail. Additional page-overflow checks cover all routes at 360, 768 and 1199 pixels.

Interaction checks cover: ownership filtering without changing the realised-sales denominator; no replay when stale/closed; no duplicate event; no crowd when inactive; profile/news navigation; reduced-motion static frames and changing work-animation frames. The initial capture reported an incorrect queue-icon URL; it was corrected before the final capture. No live backend, account or production performance test is claimed.

`activity.test.mjs` covers state/freshness gates, crowd cap, realised-share denominator, null reports, settled-only replay/deduplication/expiry and frame sampling. All pass. Type declarations are proposals, not a compiled integration.

The Impeccable launcher was unavailable for its one detector invocation (file not found). Direct structural/browser checks and an independent finish review provide evidence instead; this is not a detector pass.

## Known limits

See OUTSTANDING.md. In particular, no valid walk cycles were obtained, new actors face SW only, generated source raster is not certified native-pixel art, and the prototype uses illustrative economic data. Shipping this review pack does not mean the entire Phase 7 brief or live-game integration is complete.

Independent finish review identified mixed known/missing market reports incorrectly producing definitive shares/ranks. The helper now suppresses every share and rank until all comparable sales reports are available; regression tests cover both incomplete and complete markets. Existing complete fixture layouts were recaptured after the correction.
