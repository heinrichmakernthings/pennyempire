## Outstanding engineering and validation

The inspected engine does not implement company creation/startup quotes, upgrades, protected player market orders, supplier contracts, transport timing or tradable ownership. Capability gates and proposed view models are provided; these are engineering dependencies, not falsely completed features. Authentication and per-player isolation are part of Claude’s engine work.

The detailed management reference is intentionally pizza-specific. Selecting another startup industry previews its artwork and opportunity, but the downstream management fixture remains the pizza example. Claude must bind each actual industry’s recipes, defaults, limits and inventories; do not ship that fixture as generic gameplay. All opportunity, premises, budget and market values are illustrative. An enabled, production API is required before committing real in-game funds.

Browser tests cover the supplied reference, not engine settlement or multiplayer races. No human five-minute onboarding test or real screen-reader session has been completed. Existing Phase 1–3 world-art certification/animation gaps remain in their original reports and are not claimed fixed by this UI phase.
