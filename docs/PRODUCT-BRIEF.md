# Penny Empire — product brief v0.1

## North star
“Getting rich is fun. Staying rich is difficult. Every other player is either a customer, competitor, supplier or investment opportunity.”

## Audience and session
An approachable 60-second daily check-in with optional 10–20 minute sessions for optimisers and traders. Browser primary; mobile-responsive.

## Game pillars
1. **Place**: real city names and neighbourhoods; fictional demand, property and industry data. Players choose a city; precise real-world location is not required.
2. **Production**: own companies, set prices, staffing, input quality, output and marketing; inventory and cash matter.
3. **Interdependence**: farmers supply mills, mills supply restaurants; an order book enables player-to-player supply.
4. **Competition**: limited simulated consumer demand; compete on value, quality, availability, reputation and advertising.
5. **Capital**: fictional shares, dividends, distressed businesses, state-owned utilities and market events.
6. **Prestige**: visible city assets, supercars, penthouses and branded HQs; spend in-game coins to fight inflation.
7. **Commercial sustainability**: cosmetics, optional premium convenience and compliant display/rewarded advertising.

## First playable city
Bristol. Start with farms, flour mills, dairies, pizza restaurants, cafés, timber yards; state-run electricity, logistics, advertising and exchange.

## Core loop
Discover unmet demand → establish company → acquire inputs → configure production/pricing/staff → produce → sell to NPC consumers or players → collect profit → reinvest or buy status goods → compete for local market share.

## Progression
Neighbourhood → city → national → international. International growth requires substantial game-coin spending on market entry, logistics, marketing and premises.

## Player safety
Age-appropriate game design; transparent fees and chance mechanics; no targeted rewards designed to induce loss chasing. Pre-alpha wallet is simulated only.

## Success criteria for first playtest
New tester can create a business and execute a supply chain in under five minutes; trading settles exactly once; an NPC backstop keeps low-population markets alive; business outcomes are understandable; players make meaningful strategic choices.
