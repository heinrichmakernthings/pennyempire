# Milestones

## M0 — visual and technical foundations
- Three coherent design directions inside selected hybrid style.
- Pick the target design, freeze UI component rules and sprite scale.
- Create project scaffolding, automated lint/typecheck/tests.
- Define DB schema and economic invariants.
Exit: agreed clickable design and passing build.

## M1 — single-player Bristol vertical slice
- Interactive city overview, 6 industries, city demand finder.
- Configurable company manager and inventory.
- Tick-based production, state NPC supply/demand and inflation sinks.
Exit: create company → buy inputs → produce → sell → see profits works end-to-end.

## M2 — private multiplayer
- Invite auth, orders/trades, supplier relationships, company leaderboard.
- Atomic marketplace matching and anti-cheat controls.
Exit: two invited players trade safely and see consistent balances.

## M3 — capital markets and prestige
- Fictional shares/dividends, state companies, company news, bankruptcy.
- Prestige cosmetics and player market for non-redeemable assets.
Exit: simulated stock market operates within auditable game-coin economy.

## M4 — financial testbed
- Separate simulated GBP double-entry wallet.
- Mock deposits, cash-outs, reward budgets, reconciliation and stress scenarios.
- No actual monetary transactions.
Exit: test ledger balances reconcile and no negative funds can be created.

## M5 — closed alpha
- 10–30 invited testers, analytics, balancing, accessibility and mobile QA.
- Separate jurisdiction/licensing/payment diligence before any real-money activation.
