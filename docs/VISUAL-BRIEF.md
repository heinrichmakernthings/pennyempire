# Visual direction — pixel tycoon × market terminal

## Mood
Charming but slightly gritty 2D isometric pixel-art UK city. Contrasted with polished dark charcoal business and trading panels. A coherent game, not a banking app with decorations.

## First screen
Bristol illustrated district with clickable buildings, moving NPCs and visible signage. Collapseable right-side company dashboard; top strip for coins, portfolio and city news; left contextual navigation; desktop advertising slots only where non-intrusive. Mobile uses full-width scene and bottom nav.

## Screens to design
1. City overview and city selection.
2. Company management: price, staff, input quality, production, inventory, profits.
3. Commodity exchange: buy/sell orders, supply gaps, transaction history.
4. Fictional stock exchange: company profiles, price charts, holdings.
5. Prestige marketplace: vehicles, HQs, cosmetics, temporary capped boosts.
6. Clearly labelled simulated GBP wallet: rewards, deposit and withdrawal tests.

## Art deliverables
- Isometric building tiles for pizza shop, café, farm, mill, dairy, timber yard, utilities.
- Bristol neighbourhood tile set, roads, pavement, scenery and citizens.
- Consistent pixel scale, transparent PNG/WebP assets, UI icons, hover/selection states.
- Produce 3 composed visual explorations and select one before a full sprite pack or UI implementation.

## Avoid
Unrelated realism, clashing sprite scales, dense unreadable tables, pay-to-win pressure, fake cash winnings, advertisements covering game actions.
